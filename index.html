What it's costing you

Withdrawing isn't spending. Money you draw at an ATM has only moved from your account into your pocket — it counts as spent when you actually hand it over. So drawing yen on your FNB card and tapping that same card are never counted twice. The figure on each card above is what that account has paid out in total, which is the number to check against your banking app.

At the ATM, always choose to be charged in yen, never rands — picking rands adds a hidden 3–7% markup.
